## Brand & Style: JuJu Delivery (Mise & Margin)

This design system synthesizes high-precision operational intelligence with culinary craft. The aesthetic is clean, dense, structured, and deliberate:
- Top Title Bar: Brand identity, active unit dropdown selector, status pills.
- Left Navigation Rail: High-density routes (Pedidos, Produção, Estoque, Entregas, Produtos, Lojas, Configurações).
- Right KPI & Tools Drawer: Daily telemetry (Faturamento, Pedidos Pendentes, Alerta de Estoque) and Quick WhatsApp generator.
- Colors: Emerald (`#059669` / `#006948`), Slate (`#0f172a`), Amber (`#d97706`).
- Typography: Inter for UI & JetBrains Mono for metrics, order numbers, dates and financial values.
